JARVIS GOD'S EYE VIEW - GESTURE REPAIR

BEST FOR AN EXISTING INSTALLATION:
1. Stop God's Eye View (Ctrl+C in the server terminal).
2. Extract Mark-LVII-Gesture-Pinch-Repair-Patch.zip into your existing
   Mark-LVII project folder (the folder containing main.py).
   Confirm overwrite of the three updated files.
3. In VS Code terminal opened in that Mark-LVII folder, run:
   powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\FIX_GESTURES_WINDOWS.ps1
4. Restart the 3D globe using:
   py -3.12 start_world_map.py
5. Browser: Ctrl+Shift+R. Click JARVIS HANDS and allow camera.

CONTROLS:
- Thumb + index finger PINCH and move hand UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT: drag globe.
- Spread TWO HANDS apart: zoom IN.
- Move TWO HANDS together: zoom OUT.
- Release pinch: stop moving globe.

Check the on-screen status:
- One hand detected: bring thumb and index together.
- Pinch detected: move hand to pan.
- Two hands detected: change distance between hands.
- No hands detected: camera permissions, lighting, both hands in the frame.

NOTE: Local webcam recognition and actual Cesium camera response require a
Windows PC and cannot be verified by the offline tests in the package.
